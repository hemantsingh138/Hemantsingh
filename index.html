About Me

I’m a Trust & Safety professional with over 8 years of experience working across policy operations, quality assurance, learning and development, and cross-vendor collaboration within the digital platform industry.

Over the years, my work has focused on helping teams understand and apply complex policies, improving decision-making quality, and supporting safer online environments. Working on projects associated with major technology platforms, including Google/YouTube, has given me practical insight into the challenges of content moderation, policy enforcement, and maintaining consistency across large-scale operations.

In my current role as a Senior Trainer, I work at the intersection of policy, people, and performance. My experience includes delivering training across vendors, supporting quality improvement initiatives, contributing policy feedback for AI-assisted moderation, and translating complex policy requirements into practical guidance that teams can apply in real-world scenarios. I enjoy looking beyond individual decisions to understand the underlying gaps, identify opportunities for improvement, and build solutions that create a lasting impact.

What interests me most about this field is how policy, human judgement, technology, and risk management come together. As digital platforms evolve and AI becomes increasingly integrated into moderation workflows, I believe there is a growing need for professionals who understand both the operational realities and the principles behind effective policy enforcement.

Looking ahead, I’m interested in opportunities that allow me to build on my Trust & Safety background while expanding into areas such as policy strategy, platform governance, risk management, compliance, and AML/KYC. I’m particularly interested in applying my experience in risk identification, quality evaluation, decision-making, and process improvement to broader challenges across technology and financial services.

I see my career as an ongoing learning journey. I’m open to new challenges, new industries, and opportunities that allow me to combine my existing experience with new knowledge and skills. My goal is to grow into roles where I can contribute not only to day-to-day operations, but also to stronger policies, better processes, and more effective risk management.

This website is a space to share my professional journey, the work I’m interested in, and the opportunities I’m working towards. If you’re exploring opportunities in Trust & Safety, policy, risk, compliance, or related fields, I’d be happy to connect and exchange ideas.
