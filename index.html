I enjoy looking beyond individual decisions to understand the underlying gaps, identify opportunities for improvement, and build solutions that create a lasting impact.

Looking ahead, I’m interested in opportunities that allow me to build on my Trust & Safety background while expanding into areas such as policy strategy, platform governance, risk management, compliance, and AML/KYC. I’m particularly interested in applying my experience in risk identification, quality evaluation, decision-making, and process improvement to broader challenges across technology and financial services.

I see my career as an ongoing learning journey. I’m open to new challenges, new industries, and opportunities that allow me to combine my existing experience with new knowledge and skills. My goal is to grow into roles where I can contribute not only to day-to-day operations, but also to stronger policies, better processes, and more effective risk management.
